# Data and model access

I use SoccerNet football sequences and the pretrained Meta DINOv3 model for my experiments. I share my own processing and tracking code, small result tables and instructions in this repository.

I get the SoccerNet data and the DINOv3 checkpoint from their original sources and follow their access conditions. I keep my Hugging Face token in my local or Kaggle environment, rather than in the repository. I also keep dataset images, video files, and model weights outside Git.
